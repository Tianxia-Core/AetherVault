# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards
- Use welcoming, respectful, and constructive technical communication.
- Focus critiques strictly on cryptographic soundness, code quality, and engineering merit.
- Respect privacy and never publish sensitive operational data or credentials.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to **https://trust.rasaxmachina.com**. All reports are reviewed confidentially.
